Concordia University
Coordinated Routing for Drone-Truck Systems

Introduced the Multiple Drone and Truck Arc Routing Problem (MDTARP), coordinating a fleet of drones with a ground vehicle to service network edges within a time horizon. Formulated a multi-objective model balancing coverage and travel efficiency, and developed two heuristics — Iterated Local Search and Cluster-based Location Search — benchmarked against a commercial solver.

Qatar University
Novel thermo mechanical refrigeration cycle utilizing waste heat

Investigated a heat-driven refrigeration system that converts low-grade waste heat (≥80°C) into cooling energy (down to -10°C). Developed the thermodynamic framework for operating conditions and working fluid selection as a sustainable alternative to conventional mechanical refrigeration.
